# Improve One Harness Pass

Use this playbook for the immediately preceding harness pass. The original ordinary job supplies context but is not the optimization subject. Comparative, causal, or longitudinal claims belong to the target's evaluation owner.

## 1. Record the contract

Name the exact harness pass, its intervention or no-op, delivered artifact and proof, placement and future encounter point, telemetry source, observed avoidable tax, authority boundary, and stop condition. Do not reopen broad trajectory history without a named missing fact.

## 2. Locate the earliest avoidable tax

Find the first repeated recovery, model work, tool call, retry, wait, delivery step, cleanup step, or qualification ambiguity. Separate an unavailable capability from poor discovery and artifact quality from realized efficiency. Choose the earliest owner in the future harness execution path.

## 3. State one break-even hypothesis

Before editing, state: `If <small reversible harness-owner change>, then comparable future harness passes will avoid <observable tax> at <exact encounter point>.` Name evidence that would support or weaken it, the expected exposure count needed to repay the change, and ongoing carrying cost.

## 4. Implement and verify

Make at most one coherent change to a prompt, pass map, selective context route, tool, adapter, validator, delivery, cleanup, qualification, or retirement path. Prefer deterministic behavior over prose. Verify the mechanism, artifact identity, normal delivery, cleanup, and all active surfaces added or retired.

## 5. Leave qualification

Name one natural comparable exposed harness pass and record `pending`. A later evaluation may resolve it to `retain`, `revise`, or `remove`; do not run recursive efficiency work or claim savings from delivery alone. Prefer a measured no-op when encounter or break-even is not credible.

## Result

Report only the observed tax, earliest owner, intervention or justified no-op, encounter point, verification and delivered identity, active surfaces added and retired, break-even and carrying cost, qualification exposure, and current decision.
