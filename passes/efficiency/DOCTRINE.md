# Efficiency Pass Doctrine

This doctrine specializes `/srv/harness-ops.md` for a pass whose subject is the immediately preceding harness pass and whose beneficiary is a future harness pass. It does not repeat or replace the shared doctrine.

## 1. Audit the teaching process

Do not reopen the original ordinary job as the object of optimization. Review how the harness pass recovered its evidence, selected an owner, placed its intervention, validated and delivered it, cleaned up, and made future adoption observable.

Separate artifact quality from realized efficiency. A merged document, test, tool, or validator may be a plausible intervention without proof that a later agent encountered it or that its savings repaid its creation and maintenance cost.

## 2. Change the harness execution path

The intervention must improve how a future harness pass operates: its prompt, pass map, selective context route, telemetry, adapter, validation, delivery, cleanup, qualification, or retirement path. It should not become a second ordinary-scaffold intervention derived from the original product trajectory.

Place the learning where the future harness agent will encounter it before repeating the cost. Prefer executable behavior and deterministic guards over prose. When prose is the correct owner, it must be on the pass's required or explicitly routed path.

## 3. Measure net benefit

Measure outcome, model calls, uncached and cached input, output, tool calls and output volume, elapsed and waiting time, retries, failures, human attention, risk, and ongoing carrying cost separately when available. Do not treat provider-token shares as price-weighted cost unless the evidence includes the required rates.

State a concrete hypothesis: this owner change should remove this observable tax on these comparable future passes. Estimate how many comparable exposures are needed to repay the efficiency pass and the intervention's maintenance burden. If credible break-even is absent, prefer a measured no-op.

## 4. Avoid recursive optimization

One efficiency intervention remains active until comparable exposed evidence supports `retain`, `revise`, or `remove`. Do not stack another behavioral intervention on the same path merely because another pass can be run.

Efficiency, evaluation, repair, and control work must not recursively qualify as ordinary harness improvement. Preserve explicit episode purpose and the target's serial-intervention boundary when automation or cohort evaluation is involved.

## 5. Prove adoption and retire waste

Deterministic validation proves the mechanism and delivery identity. Later qualification must show that a comparable harness agent encountered the change, used it, reduced the targeted cost, and did not weaken ordinary outcomes, authority, proof, portability, or recovery.

Record active surfaces added, merged, removed, or moved to evidence. Delete unsuccessful, superseded, or unencountered guidance rather than preserving every optimization hypothesis. Promote genuinely cross-project principles to the shared passes source only after corroboration; keep project-specific learning with its owner.
