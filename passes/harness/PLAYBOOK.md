# Improve One Ordinary Trajectory

Use this playbook for one completed ordinary-engineering trajectory. Comparative, causal, or longitudinal claims belong to the target's evaluation owner, not this pass.

## 1. Record the contract

Name the target and delivered identity, accepted ordinary outcome and proof, observed avoidable tax, comparable future job, authority boundary, and stop condition. Use the completed trajectory as the baseline; do not replay product work merely to manufacture evidence.

## 2. Locate the earliest failed handoff

Find the first missing, stale, overloaded, undiscoverable, manual, unsafe, or weakly proved handoff. Separate context, capability, ownership, authority, proof, delivery, and worker limitations. Choose the earliest authoritative owner that can change a future execution path.

## 3. State one intervention hypothesis

Before editing, state: `If <small reversible owner change>, then a comparable future agent will <observable behavior>, because it will encounter the change at <exact point>.` Name evidence that would support or weaken it and its carrying cost.

## 4. Implement and verify

Make at most one coherent intervention through the owner's normal workflow. Prefer code, defaults, tools, errors, tests, and required routes over detached prose. Verify the mechanism, artifact identity, normal delivery, and cleanup at the claim boundary; do not call delivery behavioral qualification.

## 5. Leave qualification

Name one natural comparable exposure and record `pending`. A later exposed trajectory may resolve it to `retain`, `revise`, or `remove`; an unexposed or unrelated run cannot. Remove superseded active guidance instead of preserving every explanation.

## Result

Report only the observed tax, earliest owner, intervention or justified no-op, encounter point, verification and delivered identity, surfaces added and retired, expected carrying cost, qualification exposure, and current decision.
