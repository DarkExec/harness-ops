# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

Good doctrine changes start from evidence: a recurring execution cost, a failed handoff, or a serious
authority or safety defect. Explain the observed problem, its owner, the smallest proposed rule, the
expected effect, and what future evidence should retain, revise, or remove it.

Please:

- keep examples generic and remove private or customer information;
- avoid product-specific paths, tools, incidents, and transient policy;
- do not add a rule merely because it sounds prudent;
- run `./scripts/validate.sh`; and
- accept that no-change or removal can be the correct outcome.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under Apache-2.0.
